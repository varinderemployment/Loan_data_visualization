  Key Results

- Analyzed 1,020 trucks and 7,900+ records across 15 silos for the 
  full year 2024.
- Verified 11M+ kg of cement dispatched against a 10M+ kg pre-loading 
  weight baseline.
- Recorded 2,590 truck turnaround events (1.63K total loading hours); 
  monthly turnaround volume ranged from ~180 (April) to ~240 
  (September).
- Identified August as a bottleneck month, with average loading hours 
  nearly 4x higher than other months.
- Found a November spike in cement dispatch (~0.45M kg) against a 
  typical monthly range of 0.1M-0.2M kg.
- Flagged a ~32% data-completeness gap (missing truck type/registry 
  info) in the cleaned dataset, recommending improved data capture 
  at the source.
